Create an anonymous post

Use a nickname. Do not include a student's full name, phone number, address, private messages, exact whereabouts, password, embarrassing photo, accusation, threat, or rumor.

Safety Center

Anonymous, not consequence-free.
Do not use anonymity to bully, threaten, impersonate, expose, shame, or target another person.

Report & moderation

In a production version, every post should have a report action for bullying, threats, personal information, impersonation, or targeted accusations. Moderators can review and remove reported content.

Privacy

The public feed should expose only the anonymous display name, post text, and posting time. Keep account and moderation data separate from public posts.

Emergency rule

This app is not an emergency service. Immediate safety concerns should go to a trusted adult or appropriate emergency/school safety service.
